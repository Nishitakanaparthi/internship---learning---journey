Domain 6 - Governance , Risk , and Responsible Use:
This domain is about using claude safely, ethically , and transparently while protecting data and 
following organizational policies.

6.1 - Identify Appropriate and Inappropriate use cases:
*Identify tasks suitable for AI.
*Use claude for drafting, summarizing, and brainstorming .
*Avoid relying on AI alone for high-impact decisions.
*Maintain human judgement and review.

6.2 - Apply Privacy and Security Principles:
*Protect data and confidential informationa.
*share only necessary information.
*Anonymization - Remove identifying information.
*Pseudonymization - Replace personal identifiers with codes.

6.3 - Follow organizational AI Policies:
*Use approved AI tools.
*Follow company AI guidelines.
*Maintain access control.
*Report security incidents.

6.4 - Understand Ethical AI:
*Fairness - Avoid discrimination and bias.
*Transparency - Be clear about AI usage .
*Accountability : Take responsibility for AI-assisted decisions.
*Human oversight: Review important AI outputs.
*Safety: prevent potential harm.

Key Takeaways:
1.Use AI appropriately.
2.Protect sensitive information.
3.Follow organizational policies.
4.Ensure fairness and transparency.
5.Maintain human oversight and accountability.
