About NHS

A legacy of learning since 1966

The Nirmala Schools are established and managed by the Congregation of Missionary Sisters of the Immaculate, Vijayawada – a society registered under the Society’s Act, 1860.

  • Founded in 1966 and run by the Nirmala Sisters
  • Kindergarten to Class X under the State Syllabus
  • A private minority, unaided institution
  • A Christian atmosphere that welcomes every religion, caste and creed

Milestones through the years

From a small beginning to a school of over two thousand students.

What guides us

Emblem & Motto

A ship, a star, a safe shore

Our emblem shows a ship sailing from west to east across a deep blue ocean, guided by a bright star.

  • The ship represents human life striving to reach a safe shore – a reminder to work hard for a stronger future.
  • The star represents Nirmala Matha, the patroness of our institution.
  • The motto – Truth, Love and Service.
Principal’s Desk

Welcome to Nirmala High School

The school aims to nurture students who are caring, principled and capable, guided by respect for human dignity, fairness and justice. Our education spans academics, faith and spiritual growth, social responsibility and leadership.

Schools today face the pressures of commercialisation, declining moral values, social media and heavy competition. We will keep balancing high academic standards with strong values, under our motto of Truth, Love and Service.

— Sr. Sudha Soosai MSI, Principal
Academics

Learning for every stage

State syllabus from Kindergarten to Class X, with values-based education and activities beyond the classroom.

Pre-Primary

Play-based learning with storytelling, music, art and movement to build motor and communication skills.

Our approach

  • Small class sizes
  • Individual attention
  • A safe, stimulating environment

Primary

Language, mathematics, science and social studies, along with art, music, physical education and technology.

Our approach

  • Values-based education: teamwork, responsibility, dedication, respect
  • Interactive learning
  • A balanced curriculum

Middle School

STEM, humanities and the arts, with technology integrated into everyday learning.

Our approach

  • Analysis, problem-solving and critical thinking
  • Social and emotional growth, and leadership
  • Co-curricular activities: sports, arts and clubs

Secondary School

In-depth learning of core subjects including mathematics, science and languages.

Our approach

  • Board exam and competitive test preparation
  • Career guidance
  • Inquiry-based learning, debates and projects
  • A mix of academics, sports, arts and culture

Fee Instructions

School fees are payable for three terms of the year.

1st TermJunePay on or before the 25th
2nd TermOctoberPay on or before the 25th
3rd TermJanuaryPay on or before the 25th
  • All fees are strictly payable in advance, i.e. on or before the 25th of every term. No payment of fees will be accepted from the 26th of every term.
  • Any pupil whose fees are in arrears shall not be allowed to sit for the regular examinations.
  • Reduction in fees is granted in special cases to poor and deserving students, but the concession will be discontinued if the application and conduct are not satisfactory.
  • When a child has once been withdrawn, fee must be paid again on re-admission.
  • Fees once paid are not refundable.
