Domain - 7: Troubleshooting and Optimization
This domain focuses on improving Claude’s performance by identifying problems, using feedback and
optimizing workflows.

7.1 - Diagnose Underperforming Prompts And Poor Outputs
Identify unclear prompts , missing context , and inaccurate responses. Improve prompts by providing clear 
Instructions and context.

7.2 - Adjust Your Approach Based On Feedback 
Use user feedback to identify problems , refine prompts and improve the quality and relevance  of claude's
responses.

7.3 - Optimize Workflows for Efficiency
Improve productivity by using reusable prompts, reducing repetitive tasks, breaking complex tasks into 
smaller steps.
