Domain 4 - workflow Integration and Solution Design

Overview 
This domain focuses on integrating claude into existing workflows, identifying business requirements,
designing solutions and improving process using AI.

Objective 4.1 - Analyze requirements  and identify use cases:
Understanding business problems and identity where claude can provide useful solutions.

Objective 4.2 - Research, Planning, and process optimization
Use claude to support research, organize information , plan activities, and improve workflow efficiency

Objective 4.3 - Support solution design, development, and iteration
Use claude to design solutions, create prototypes, test outputs, collect feedback, and improve results

Objective 4.4 - Integrate claude into existing  workflows: augment or redesign
Understand how claude can support existing tasks or redesign workflows for better productivity 

objective 4.5 - Communicate claude's value and limitations to stakeholders
Explain claude's benefits , limoitations, risks, and expected outcomes clearly to stakeholders

Practical Learning 
- Identifying real world business use cases
- Improving workflows using claude
- Designing and refining AI solutions
- Integrating claude into daily tasks

Learning Outcome
Understand how to apply claude to real world business workflows and support effective AI solution design
